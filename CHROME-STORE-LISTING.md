## Detailed Description

📱 **WhatsApp Contacts Exporter** — Export your WhatsApp contacts in seconds.

No more manually copying contacts one by one. Open WhatsApp Web, click export, and get a clean CSV or JSON file ready for Excel, Google Sheets, or your CRM.

**📋 How It Works:**
1. Open WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com)
2. Click the extension icon
3. Choose CSV or JSON format
4. Download your contacts file — done!

**✨ Key Features:**

• **One-Click Export** — Extract all visible contacts instantly
• **CSV Format** — Opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers
• **JSON Format** — Perfect for developers and CRM imports
• **Smart Filtering** — Export only contacts, only groups, or both
• **Contact Details** — Name, phone number, contact type, last seen time
• **Group Detection** — Automatically identifies groups vs individual contacts
• **Phone Number Extraction** — Pulls phone numbers when visible
• **Excel-Ready CSV** — Includes BOM header for proper UTF-8 encoding
• **Scroll to Load** — Tip: scroll through your chat list first to load more contacts

**👥 Perfect For:**

• **Small Business Owners** — Back up your customer contacts
• **Sales Professionals** — Build prospect lists from WhatsApp leads
• **Phone Migration** — Moving to a new phone? Export first.
• **CRM Import** — Get contacts into HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, etc.
• **Event Organizers** — Export group member lists
• **Data Backup** — Keep a safe copy of your contacts

**🔒 100% Private & Secure:**

Your data NEVER leaves your browser. This extension:
• Does NOT send data to any server
• Does NOT require an account
• Does NOT use analytics or tracking
• Processes everything locally on your computer
• Has NO external dependencies

We take privacy seriously. Your WhatsApp contacts are personal — they stay on your machine.

**💡 Pro Tips:**
• Scroll through your entire chat list before exporting to load all contacts
• Use the filter checkboxes to export only what you need
• CSV files open directly in Excel with proper formatting
